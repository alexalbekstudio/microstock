5 mistakes Etsy sellers make (and how to avoid them)

Etsy is an excellent platform for small sellers — over 90 million active buyers, simple setup, low costs. But **many sellers make the same mistakes** that cost them money, time and customers.

In this article, I'll show you the **5 most common mistakes** and **how to avoid them** — based on experience with hundreds of Etsy sellers.

Mistake #1: Stock out of control

Problem: You sell the same product on Etsy and Instagram. You manage stock in Excel. When you sell on Etsy, you forget to reduce the quantity. The next customer on Instagram orders — you don't have it in stock.

Consequence: Lost customer, bad review, lost revenue.

Solution: Use **one system** for all channels. When you sell anywhere, stock is updated everywhere. MicroStock does this automatically.

Mistake #2: No analytics

Problem: You know you sold 50 items this month, but **you don't know which ones**. You don't know which channel brings the most, which product loses money, which customer returns.

Consequence: You run your business blind. You sell a product that costs you more than you earn, without even knowing.

Solution: Track **4 key numbers**:

  • Revenue by channel (Etsy vs Instagram vs WhatsApp)
  • Profit by product (after fees and costs)
  • Margin (what's left from each sale)
  • Top customers (who brings the most revenue)

MicroStock calculates all these numbers automatically — no Excel, no formulas.

Mistake #3: Writing invoices by hand

Problem: For every order, you open Word, type the same data over and over, save a PDF with a weird name, send it to the customer...

Consequence: You lose 5-10 minutes per order. With 50 orders per month, that's **8 hours of lost time**.

Solution: Use a tool that **automatically generates invoices** from orders. One click — a PDF with your logo, in your currency, with all data.

Mistake #4: Not tracking purchase costs

Problem: You know how much you sold, but **you don't know how much it costs you**. Purchase cost changes, Etsy fee is 6.5%, shipping costs vary...

Consequence: You think you have 30% margin, but actually you have 5%. Or worse — you sell **below cost**.

Solution: Use a **pricing calculator** that takes into account:

  • Product cost
  • Etsy fee (6.5%)
  • Shipping cost
  • Tax (if VAT registered)
  • Desired margin

MicroStock has a built-in calculator — select a channel, enter your margin, get a **recommended price**.

Mistake #5: Forgetting customers

Problem: You sell a product, ship it, forget. You don't track who returns, who's satisfied, who brings the most.

Consequence: You miss the chance to **retain a customer** — and retention is 5x cheaper than finding a new one.

Solution: Keep a **CRM for customers** — name, contact, order history. When you get a new order from the same customer, you know they're valuable.

MicroStock shows **top 10 customers** by revenue — you immediately see who to focus on.

Conclusion

Etsy is an excellent channel, but **only if you run it smartly**. If you make these 5 mistakes, you lose money, time and customers.
