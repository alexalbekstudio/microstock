Frequently asked questions.

Do I need to install anything?

No. MicroStock runs in your browser. Open the link, sign in, and start. You can add it as an app on your phone (PWA) with one click.

Can I export my data?

Yes. You can export data to CSV or JSON at any time. Your data is yours — it's not locked in.

What happens if I cancel my subscription?

Your data stays saved for 30 days. You can come back anytime or export everything before canceling.

Is it secure?

Yes. SSL encryption, HTTPS, data in the EU (Frankfurt). Every user has their own account with a password. Regular database backups.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. MicroStock is a PWA — install it as an app on your home screen. It works offline for basic tasks too.

How do I get started?

Click "Try free for 14 days," create an account and start. Setup takes 5 minutes.
